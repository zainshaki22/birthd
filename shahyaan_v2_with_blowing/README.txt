PROJECT SHAHYAAN.EXE v2.1 — Original v2 + microphone candle challenge

1. Deploy the entire folder to Vercel (HTTPS) for microphone permissions.
2. Open the website on a phone or computer. Complete the birth-year challenge and quiz.
3. At the candle stage, press ENABLE MIC & BLOW, allow permission, and blow steadily toward the microphone until all 21 candles go out.
4. The birthday message and gallery unlock automatically when all candles are out.
5. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js together. Add photos/memory1.jpg, memory2.jpg, memory3.jpg for the gallery.
6. The microphone is processed locally, never recorded or uploaded. There is deliberately no tap-to-extinguish shortcut.
7. Microphone sensitivity varies by device; if detection is difficult, move closer to the mic.
